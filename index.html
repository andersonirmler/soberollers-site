FAQ

Frequently askedquestions

IS HELMET REQUIRED TO SKATE?
YES! We care about your safety!
CAN I PARTICIPATE IF I AM NOT AN EXPERIENCED SKATER?
Skater Migration is for experienced skaters that feel comfortable, skating on the street with cars, skating at least 15 miles with bridges, stop lights and potholes. We do not recommend new skaters. If you don’t feel comfortable street skating, you can bike with us!
WHERE DOES THE SKATES START?
At the board walk behind the host hotel (63 st and Collins)
WHERE CAN I PARK?
Parking is on 72nd street. Parking is $1.25 per hour. You can take the boardwalk south to the host hotel.
DO I NEED INSURANCE FOR SKATING?
Skating has its risks and all participants will have to sign a waiver acknowledging that we all skate at our own risk. You may want to check with your own health insurance provider to see if you are covered for accidents outside your area. If you are coming from outside the US, you should check that your medical insurance covers you while on foreign travel.
SHOULD I BRING NIGHT LIGHTS FOR SKATING? BLINKING LIGHTS, FLASHING WHEELS, FLURO VEST, SEQUINED JUMPSUIT, ETC.
The easier it is for you to be seen at night, the better. Blinkies are required for the night skates. Light color/reflective clothing is advised. Night skates are not the time to try out your new black spandex skinsuit! Head lamps are always recommended at night. And as always, helmets are required. The sequined jumpsuit is not required, but may be a fun outfit to wear as you glide through Times Square. Remember, you are skating at your own risk.
ARE BIKERS WELCOME?
Bikers are welcomed as long as they keep in mind that skaters kick-out. Therefore, bikers must give skaters adequate space at all times, ride in the back, in front of skaters, or to the side.
WHAT WILL BE RAFFLED?
Thanks to our sponsors everything from shirts, wheels, gear, and even skates! ( Tickets $5 each, or 5 for $20)

02 / The weekend

Come for the skates. Stay for the people.

Hosted by SoBe Rollers, Skater Migration brings skaters together for four days of group routes, fitness sessions, sightseeing and new friendships in Miami Beach. This is a recreational street-skating weekend for intermediate to advanced skaters. Be comfortable stopping, turning, navigating hills and skating in a group during the day and at night. February 18–21, 2027 · Miami Beach, Florida Meeting point: 4333 Collins Avenue

04 / Before you go

Ready for the streets?

Skate ready

This weekend is for intermediate to advanced street skaters. Be comfortable stopping, turning, navigating hills and uneven surfaces, and skating in a group. It is not a beginner lesson.

Gear & check-in

A signed waiver and event wristband are required. Helmets and wrist guards are mandatory; full protective gear is recommended. Bring water and lights for night skates. Cyclists are welcome: give skaters space to move laterally.

Meet on Collins Avenue

4333 Collins Avenue, Miami Beach, FL 33140. Confirm arrival details, routes and hotel arrangements with the organizer. The schedule is tentative; check RaceResult for final updates.

About the event

Welcome to the premier and largest recreational skating event in the United States, proudly hosted by Soberollers and #Skatewithus! Escape the winter and embark on a thrilling four-day journey filled with skating, sunshine, sightseeing around Miami, and the opportunity to forge new friendships! Before you hit the pavement, remember: sign the waiver, secure your wristband, and gear up with essential protective equipment. We strongly advise the use of wrist-guards, elbow pads, and knee guards for a safe and enjoyable experience. Helmets are non-negotiable – your safety is paramount. Skater Migration isn't for the beginner skater; it's tailored for intermediate to advanced skaters who effortlessly navigate street skating in groups, whether under the sun or stars. Mastered the art of stopping and turning on all terrains, including hills, while adhering to traffic laws for an unforgettable skating adventure. Rolling on all kinds of wheels? You're welcome! Cyclists, join the fun but keep in mind that skaters kick out to the side. To ensure harmony, bikers must maintain a respectful distance – ride behind, in front, or alongside, giving skaters the space they need. Get ready to roll into the ultimate skating experience, where every turn is an opportunity for excitement and connection!
